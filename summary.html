Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy = (Your Score ÷ Total Marks) × 100. Grades: Critical <35%, Weak 35–49%, Average 50–64%, Good 65–84%, Excellent ≥85%.

Q: Is my data saved?

Yes — all data is stored in your browser's localStorage and persists after closing the tab.

Q: Can I add the same subject multiple times?

Yes! Each test entry is tracked individually on the chart — you'll see your progress over time.

Q: How do I reset my data?

Go to the Home page and click the "Reset All Data" button. This permanently deletes all logs and test results.
