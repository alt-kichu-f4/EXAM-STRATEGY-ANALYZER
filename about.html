What is Exam Strategy Analyzer?

A web-based tool built for students to organise study sessions, track mock test performance, and identify weak areas before exams.


The idea came from the common problem students face — studying a lot but not knowing if they are studying the right things. This tool helps bridge that gap.

Features at a Glance

  • 📌 Log study sessions by subject and topic
  • 📝 Record mock test scores and track accuracy
  • 📊 Multi-line chart with per-subject colour coding
  • 🎯 5-zone performance grading (Critical → Excellent)
  • 💡 Personalised study suggestions based on your scores
  • 🔖 Summary table of all subject performances with grade
  • 🌙 Dark and Light mode with smooth transitions
  • 💬 Daily motivational quotes to keep you going

Development Timeline

  • Week 1: Project planning and HTML structure
  • Week 2: CSS styling and responsive design
  • Week 3: JavaScript functionality and localStorage
  • Week 4: Testing, debugging, and final deployment
